Build with AI: shoot

Keys

  • ⌃ ⌥ ⌘ 1 terminal, you, page
  • ⌃ ⌥ ⌘ 2 terminal, page
  • ⌃ ⌥ ⌘ 3 terminal, you, phone
  • ⌃ ⌥ ⌘ 4 terminal, phone
  • ⌃ ⌥ ⌘ P pause or resume, same file
  • ⌃ ⌥ ⌘ S stop

Move the terminal, page and mirror anywhere. They hold their places in the frame, and other apps stay out. Resizing one changes it in the frame. A window closed during a take stays blank until you stop and run shoot NAME again, which also resumes after a break: same page, Claude continues, new take in the same inbox folder. Stopping leaves the windows open; end the mirror by unplugging the phone or with ⌘ Q in its window.

A take is one file with nothing to sync, so skip the camera lines, both claps, Auto Align and the backup-track mute. To publish, run ship NAME SHELF, which moves the draft to its shelf on the site.

While recording — the five glances

  • Red frame still up (recording)
  • AF box sitting on your eye (focus)
  • No zebra stripes on your face (exposure)
  • Audio meters moving (sound)
  • Plug icon + battery steady (power)
